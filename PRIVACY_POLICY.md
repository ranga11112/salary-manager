# Privacy Policy for Salary Manager: Budget & Loans

**Last updated: September 24, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how Salary Manager handles your information when you use our mobile application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

**Salary Manager does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.**

All data entered into the app (salary records, transactions, loan details, profile information) is stored **locally on your device only**. We do not have access to this data.

### Data Stored Locally:
- Salary and income records
- Transaction history
- Loan and advance records
- Profile information (name, photo)
- App preferences and settings

---

## 2. Data Sharing

We do **not** share any data with third parties. Your financial information never leaves your device.

---

## 3. Permissions Used

| Permission | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| INTERNET | Required by the WebView framework |
| USE_BIOMETRIC | Optional fingerprint/face unlock for app security |
| POST_NOTIFICATIONS | For optional local reminder notifications |
| CAMERA | For capturing receipt photos (optional) |

We do **not** request SMS, call log, location, or any other sensitive permissions.

---

## 4. Data Security

All data is stored locally using the device's built-in storage mechanisms. We recommend enabling your device's screen lock for additional security. The app also supports an optional PIN/Biometric lock.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for users **18 years of age and older**. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

---

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the Last updated date at the top of this page.
